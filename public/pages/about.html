Application Sections Guide

📊 Dashboard

Get a high-level overview of system metrics, pending tasks, recent inventory movements, and low stock alerts.

📦 Products

Manage the product catalog, create new items, organize items by tags, and view consolidated inventory levels.

📥 Receipts

Manage inbound orders from suppliers. Track expected deliveries, record physical item reception into the warehouse, and finalize shipments.

📤 Deliveries

Manage outbound customer orders. Pick items from the warehouse and record fulfilled orders.

🔄 Transfers

Move stock between different warehouses or sub-sections to ensure inventory is correctly placed.

⚙️ Adjustments

Correct inventory discrepancies found during audits (e.g. damaged goods, miscounts). Includes strict variance tracking.

📋 Move History

A complete, immutable audit trail of every single inventory transaction in the system.

👥 Employees

Admin panel to manage user accounts, assign roles, and activate/deactivate staff.

Role Access Control (RBAC)

StockMaster enforces strict security rules. Your assigned role determines what actions you can perform.

ADMIN

System Administrator

Full unrestricted access to all features. The only role that can manage employee accounts, assign roles, and override transaction restrictions.

MANAGER

Inventory Manager

Can approve stock adjustments, cancel orders, and oversee all warehouse operations. Cannot create or modify employee accounts.

INVENTORY_CLERK

Inventory Clerk

Responsible for data entry and purchase/sales orders. Can create Receipts and Deliveries in DRAFT mode and confirm them to WAITING, but cannot physically receive goods.

WAREHOUSE_WORKER

Warehouse Worker

Responsible for physical stock handling. Can perform Transfers, record physical received/delivered quantities, and mark orders as READY or DONE. Cannot create new orders or cancel them.
